# Phá lấu · English narration

**Dish ID:** `pha-lau`

## Kịch bản lời dẫn

Phá lấu is a savory stew of beef offal and aromatic spices, often enjoyed with bread. Tear off a piece of bread and dip it into the broth. Ask about ingredients and sauces if you have food allergies.
