# Bánh khọt · English narration

**Dish ID:** `banh-khot`

## Kịch bản lời dẫn

Bánh khọt are small, crisp rice pancakes, often topped with shrimp. Wrap one in fresh greens and dip it in fish sauce. If you have a seafood allergy, ask about the topping and dipping sauce.
