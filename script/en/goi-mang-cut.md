# Green mangosteen chicken salad · English narration

**Dish ID:** `goi-mang-cut`

## Kịch bản lời dẫn

Green mangosteen chicken salad combines sliced chicken, crisp unripe mangosteen, and Vietnamese coriander. Toss gently to keep the fruit crisp. It is seasonal and may contain peanuts; ask the vendor about availability and ingredients.
