# Bánh bèo bì · English narration

**Dish ID:** `banh-beo`

## Kịch bản lời dẫn

These soft rice cakes are served with shredded pork skin, mung beans, fresh greens, and fish sauce. Add the fish sauce a little at a time, then enjoy them while still soft. The dish may include peanuts; ask the vendor if you have an allergy.
