# Bột chiên · English narration

**Dish ID:** `bot-chien`

## Kịch bản lời dẫn

Bột chiên is pan-fried rice and tapioca flour cake, often served with egg and green onion. Add a little soy sauce and some shredded green papaya. Ask the vendor about ingredients if you have an egg or soy allergy.
